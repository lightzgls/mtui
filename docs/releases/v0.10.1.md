MTUI v0.10.1 removes mouse hover highlighting.

Moving the pointer no longer recolors or underlines cards, links, tabs, or controls. Clicking, right-click actions, scrolling, and dragging the progress and volume bars continue to work, including the 5% volume controls.

On Windows v0.10.0, open **Settings → Updates** to install this patch and restart. Installer and portable downloads are also available below.
