MTUI v0.10.2 improves Discord cover art and adds optional tray-click playback control.

- Discord presence now prefers native square YouTube Music artwork, avoiding the padding in rectangular video thumbnails when square artwork is available.
- On Windows, enable **Settings → Integrations → Click tray icon to stop** to stop playback with a left-click. The preference is saved across restarts and is off by default. Use **right-click → Show the player** to open MTUI while it is enabled.

On Windows v0.10.0 and later, open **Settings → Updates** to install this patch and restart. Installer and portable downloads are also available below.
