MTUI v0.10.0 adds in-app Windows updates and makes browsing work better with the mouse.

- Open **Settings → Updates** to check for a stable release, then choose **Update to v…** to download, verify, install, and restart. Installed and portable copies update in place and preserve your settings and account data.
- Click songs and catalogue cards on Artist pages. Previous/next buttons let you browse each section without arrow keys; right-click opens actions for the item you clicked.
- Related now uses cover thumbnails and stacked track details, matching the rest of the player. Section headings and spacing do not activate items.
- Hover highlights clickable cards, links, tabs, and controls without changing selection or starting playback.
- Use the volume −/+ buttons or wheel over volume for precise 5% changes. Dragging the volume bar snaps to 5%, and rapid clicks accumulate correctly.
- Extend the footer color into the native Windows window's leftover pixels so resizing does not leave a separate strip beside or below it. Keep control padding inside the footer.
- Closing the Windows window continues playback in the tray; forcibly ending its task also stops the detached player. Unexpected terminal helper termination no longer leaves audio playing.

Validated with 559 passing isolated tests, strict Clippy checks, and rendered UI previews. Checks include Artist mouse paging, recommendation hit areas, rapid volume changes, close/quit state handling, and update download checksums and truncation. Both published Windows packages were downloaded and verified, and an isolated portable application successfully replaced itself and restarted through the updater helper. Physical monitor transitions, the taskbar menu, and installation through the real Windows installer still need a desktop check.

Downloads:

- **MTUI-0.10.0-Setup-x64.exe** — Windows installer.
- **MTUI-0.10.0-windows-x64-portable.exe** — single-file portable app.

v0.9.x requires this upgrade to gain the new updater. From v0.10.0, future releases can be installed from Settings.
