MTUI v0.11.0 adds a full YouTube Music Library, optional Japanese romaji, and a new default app icon.

- Open **Library** from the top bar, App Menu, or with **L**. Browse All, Playlists, Songs, Albums, Artists, and Subscriptions, with saved selection positions, paging, sorting, and refresh. Sign in with **M** to access your collections.
- On Lyrics, select **Romaji** or press **t** to request community-reviewed Japanese readings from AMLL TTML DB. Original lyrics remain available. Romaji follows playback only when the original words and timing match the current recording; otherwise it appears as unsynced text.
- The new mint strip icon is the single default for the app and Windows installer. Tray strips rise during playback and smoothly settle into a silver strip when paused or stopped. The icon picker and alternate icons have been removed; upgrading preserves other preferences.
- Enable **Settings → Playback → Precision volume** to adjust volume by 1% per wheel step over the current cover. Playback volume is capped at 100%.
- **Click tray icon to pause / resume** now keeps the current song and position. Existing tray-click preferences stay enabled after upgrading. Use **right-click → Show the player** to open MTUI.
- Now Playing prefers native square YouTube Music cover art, with a video-thumbnail fallback. Discord follows the same artwork choice.

Validated on Windows with 605 passing isolated tests, all-feature tests and strict Clippy, ten stress passes at eight test threads, and both local AAC decoder/recovery probes. The native tray animation test and read-only live Library and romaji probes also passed. Settings previews were checked at normal and narrow sizes. Live account-writing tests remain excluded.

From v0.10.0 onward, open **Settings → Updates** to install and restart. Installer and portable downloads are available below.
