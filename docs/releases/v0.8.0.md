MTUI v0.8.0 improves the TUI, playback recovery, and YouTube Music listening-history delivery. Windows continues to use one app executable, with sign-in and console hosting built in.

### Interface and controls

- Reworked Home and Now Playing around artwork, a compact top bar, player tabs, and persistent playback controls.
- Grouped Settings into Playback, Appearance, and Integrations, with explicit choice pickers. Simplified the Ctrl+K menu and its keyboard and mouse navigation.
- Added progress-bar dragging, mute and volume controls, right-click item actions, and manual playback retry.
- Removed native console scrollbars and the black strip below the footer. Replaced playback symbols that appeared as missing glyphs in the Windows console.

### Playback

- Prefer validated, complete AAC streams and decode only audio packets in combined video/audio fallbacks.
- Preserve playback position and pause state when replacing a failed or truncated stream. Retry bypasses failed cached URLs, and byte-range splicing requires matching stream metadata.
- Keep streaming memory bounded with a 1 MiB download buffer and 2 MiB packet cache.

### Listening history

- Report signed-in playback after 30 seconds of actual listening, excluding pauses, buffering, and seek jumps.
- Persist reports before delivery, retry them in the background and on restart, and confirm the song appears in YouTube Music history before acknowledging delivery.
- Recover unverified reports from the previous 24 hours when upgrading. Full listening durations remain in the local journal; account history writes require YouTube history to be enabled.

### Project structure

- Separate settings state, dialogs, menus, shell layout, audio decoding, and history delivery into focused modules.
- Add the requirements, analysis, design, and TUI architecture decision documents.

### Windows downloads

- **MTUI-0.8.0-Setup-x64.exe**: per-user installer with Start-menu integration.
- **MTUI-0.8.0-windows-x64-portable.exe**: the single-file portable app.

This release does not establish the 50 MiB memory target or complete every planned feature in the design documents.
