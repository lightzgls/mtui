MTUI v0.11.9 adds playlist editing and a local listening history, and improves playback on slow connections.

- **Create and edit YouTube Music playlists.** Create a private playlist from Library, or create one while saving the current song. Open a playlist you own to rename it, remove songs, or move songs up and down.
- **Revisit songs from Listening history.** Open **Library → History** to see recorded plays newest first, with their local play time. Press Enter or double-click to play a song again. The list works without signing in.
- **Play more reliably on slow connections.** Audio downloads can keep going while data is still arriving, even if a chunk takes longer than ten seconds.
- **See video artwork in Discord.** Rich Presence uses the video's thumbnail when YouTube Music has no cover.
- **Share playback diagnostics.** **Menu → Copy diagnostics** gives you MTUI and playback tool versions, your platform, and a short playback error summary without raw logs, account data, or listening history.

Update from **Settings → Updates** on Windows and writable Linux installations. On Linux, if you installed MTUI through a system package manager, update through that package manager.
