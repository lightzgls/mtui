MTUI v0.11.2 fixes playback getting stuck after seeking.

- A stalled seek can be interrupted by another seek, a new song, Stop, or Quit. Playback remains usable in the same session.
- Replacement stream URLs can reach a seek waiting for bytes. Failed seeks time out instead of leaving the player stuck.
- Preserve pause when seeking, and finish progress-bar endpoint seeks without fetching unread audio.
- Retain up to eight reusable connections per host for YouTube Music metadata and playback resolution.

Validated on Windows with 596 passing isolated tests, strict Clippy, and three local AAC regressions covering decoding, recovery, stalled middle seeks, switching songs, pause, Stop, and shutdown.

Open **Settings → Updates** to install and restart. Installer and portable downloads are available below.
