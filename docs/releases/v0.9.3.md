MTUI v0.9.3 improves Home browsing, the player layout, and audio output switching.

- Open Home with large cover cards and compact Quick picks, with clearer spacing and window margins.
- Browse each Home section with clickable previous/next buttons, then click a cover or caption to play or open it.
- Keep the song title and artist below the cover, show them once on Playing, and center the playback buttons.
- Use font-aware navigation symbols and share the supported Playing symbol with the player and queue. Unknown fonts use readable text and ASCII fallbacks.
- Expand lyrics into a full-width reading view and use Follow lyrics to control synced scrolling.
- Follow changes to the system default audio output, including unplugging headphones, while preserving song position, pause, and volume. Retry temporary output failures automatically.

Validated with 547 passing default tests, strict Clippy checks, and rendered UI previews. Audio routing tests cover default changes, reconnection retries, and playback state; physical headphone unplugging still requires a hardware check.

Downloads:

- **MTUI-0.9.3-Setup-x64.exe** — Windows installer.
- **MTUI-0.9.3-windows-x64-portable.exe** — single-file portable app.

Fully exit MTUI from the tray before upgrading or replacing the portable executable.
